Compare your home network’s roaming offer with a local SIM or travel eSIM. Check whether you need only data or also a local phone number, and whether your phone supports the plan.

Before you leave home

  1. Check that your phone is unlocked and supports eSIM if you choose that option.
  2. Read the plan’s activation rule before installing. Installation and the start of validity are not always the same event.
  3. Save the setup instructions where you can find them again.
  4. Download useful maps and save your hotel address as a fallback.

On arrival, follow the provider’s instructions for selecting mobile data and roaming settings. Calls or messages on your home SIM may still incur your home provider’s charges.

Dubai is one stop on your trip?

A UAE plan does not automatically cover Oman, Saudi Arabia or your next destination. Check the exact country list on a regional plan, or choose separate country plans.

Mobile data also does not guarantee access to every internet calling service. If calls matter to your trip, check the service and plan before relying on it.
