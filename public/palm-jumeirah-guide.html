Palm Jumeirah: The View, the beach or Aquaventure?

Choose one main reason to visit the Palm. The observation deck, beachfront and Atlantis attractions are spread out; they are not a single walk-through attraction.

Reviewed 30 October 2026

Three different days on the Palm

The View at The Palm is the choice for seeing the island’s shape from above. Palm West Beach suits a meal and a seafront outing. Aquaventure is a ticketed waterpark visit that we would give most of a day.

The trunk and outer crescent cover a large area. Check the precise entrance for your booking and plan transport between stops. Passing a resort does not mean its pools or beach are open to non-guests.

Choosing your time

For The View, we would favour clear daylight if the shape of the island is the main draw. Compare the operator’s peak and off-peak slots before paying. Sunset is an option, not a guarantee of better visibility.

For the beach or waterpark, the cooler months make a longer outdoor day easier. In summer, build in shade and indoor breaks; check the venue’s current operating conditions.

How much fits in one day?

The View plus a meal can fit alongside Marina and JBR. Aquaventure plus a second major attraction is a different pace. Check ride restrictions and what your ticket includes before deciding a family needs the largest bundle.
