Museum of the Future: check the closure before planning

At our 30 October 2026 review, the museum’s own website says its exhibitions are temporarily closed. Do not build your itinerary around an exhibition ticket until reopening is confirmed.

Reviewed 30 October 2026

What is currently confirmed

The official site says the museum is preparing its next chapter and that the exhibitions are unavailable. Its retail store and selected experiences are separate from the main exhibitions. An event listing is not confirmation that the full museum has reopened.

Use the official website for an updated status before booking. We have not assumed a reopening date or carried older ticket prices into this guide.

Seeing the building

The ring-shaped building and Arabic calligraphy are major parts of its appeal. If you want an exterior photograph, check current access arrangements and keep to areas open to visitors. An exterior stop is different from a museum visit.

The landmark is on Sheikh Zayed Road near Emirates Towers. For an outdoor photograph, we would choose cooler morning or late-afternoon conditions rather than the hottest part of the day.

What to plan instead

For a first trip, use Dubai Frame or spend longer around the Creek and souks. If your main interest is museums, our Abu Dhabi guide includes Louvre Abu Dhabi.

The seven-day itinerary keeps this as a conditional option, rather than counting a closed attraction as a full day.
