Where to stay in Dubai for a first visit

Start with Downtown for landmark sightseeing, Deira or Bur Dubai for the Creek and old-city districts, and Marina or JBR for a waterfront stay. Then compare the actual journey from each hotel.

Reviewed 30 October 2026

Make a shortlist of three hotels

Use the same dates, occupancy and cancellation terms. Mark your two or three must-do activities on a map, then compare journeys from each hotel.

A room that costs less can still create a more expensive trip if every outing needs a long taxi ride. A central-looking pin can also hide a lengthy walk around a road, mall or station entrance.

Check the details the listing photo cannot show

  • Which facilities are included, and which cost extra?
  • Does the room setup work for everyone in your group?
  • Can you check in at your arrival time?
  • Is the route to public transport manageable with your group and in the season you are visiting?
  • Are taxes, breakfast and cancellation terms included in the comparison?

Match the area to the length of your stay

For a short visit, keeping your main activities close together matters more than seeing every district. For a longer break, you have room to dedicate separate days to different parts of the city.
