Three days in Dubai: a first-visit outline

Use one day for the Creek and Old Dubai, one for Downtown, and one for the waterfront or your main special-interest activity. Treat this as three full days, not an arrival afternoon plus two days.

Reviewed 30 October 2026

Day 1: the Creek and Old Dubai

Start with Al Fahidi Historical Neighbourhood, then build the rest of your day around the Creek. An abra crossing and the souks can fit into the same area-focused outing.

Choose a place for lunch and leave time to look around. You do not need a ticketed attraction in every gap. Check current transport details and venue hours before setting out.

Day 2: Downtown

Group Dubai Mall and the Burj Khalifa area together. If an observation deck is important to you, choose a specific ticket and time slot from the operator before arranging the rest of the day.

Decide what you want to spend time on: the view, shopping, an indoor attraction or a longer meal. Trying to do all of them can make one district feel rushed.

Day 3: waterfront or a personal favourite

Marina and JBR suit a day built around a waterfront walk and beach time. For a different trip, use this day for a museum, a booked activity or a quieter hotel day instead.

For a tour outside the city, check the operator’s pickup area, return time, included activities and cancellation policy. Do not squeeze a return-time-dependent excursion immediately before your flight.

Adjust the outline to your trip

  • Hot weather: keep the middle of the day flexible and favour indoor stops.
  • Children or mixed mobility: reduce the number of transitions and check access directly with venues.
  • Two days: choose the two areas you care about most. Do not compress all three.
  • Departure day: plan airport travel first, then fit in anything else.

Check when to visit and where to stay before turning this outline into bookings.
