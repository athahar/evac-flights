Sharjah: heritage lanes, art and the lagoon

Choose Heart of Sharjah for a history-focused start, then add one art experience or a lagoon outing. Sharjah’s desert and east-coast places belong on separate days.

Reviewed 30 October 2026

Walk through Heart of Sharjah

The heritage district brings together restored buildings, museums and souks. Start with the exhibitions that interest you, then leave space for the streets between them. Souq Al Arsah is a useful part of a heritage-focused visit, rather than another shopping mall stop.

Our preference would be a cooler morning for walking, followed by an indoor museum. Check individual opening days and Friday hours before travelling from Dubai.

Rain Room or Al Noor Island?

Rain Room is an indoor art installation with timed visits. Al Noor Island combines landscaped outdoor space with art and the Butterfly House. Choose according to your interests and the weather, and check which elements your ticket includes.

A lagoon walk is more comfortable later in the day. You do not need to visit both attractions to have a complete day; leave time to sit down for a meal and get back without rushing.

Sharjah extends well beyond the city

Mleiha is an inland archaeology and desert trip. Khorfakkan, Al Rafisah and Shees are on the east-coast side of the emirate. They do not sit beside the city centre, despite sharing the same emirate name.

For a short Dubai stay, make Sharjah a replacement day rather than an extra evening outing. Traffic affects the return journey, so avoid a tightly timed Dubai booking afterwards. The two-week route groups the east-coast stops separately.
