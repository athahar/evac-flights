Dubai travel guide/When to go

When is the best time to visit Dubai?

For a first trip with plenty of time outdoors, start with November to March. October and April can also work if you plan around warmer days.

Visit Dubai describes October to April as the main outdoor season. Summer offers can make a trip appealing, but a lower room price does not make a long outdoor itinerary comfortable. Choose the season for your activities first.

Compare actual dates, not just months

Price the same trip on two or three sets of dates: flights, the same room type, transport and the attractions you would actually book. A hotel offer has little value if the flight cost cancels it out.

Look at the official events calendar before paying. A concert, exhibition or holiday can change availability on particular dates. Check cancellation terms as carefully as the headline price.

If you visit in summer

Build a day that works even if you skip the outdoor part. Put a museum, indoor attraction or relaxed meal in the middle of the day, and keep transport between stops straightforward.

For a beach-focused stay, check what the hotel actually includes: beach access, pool hours, shade and transport. For city sightseeing, compare the walk from your accommodation to the station, not just its distance on a map.

Before you book

  • Check the forecast close to departure. Seasonal guidance is not a forecast for your dates.
  • Check opening dates for seasonal attractions directly with the venue.
  • Choose an area that suits your plans using our neighbourhood guide.
  • Leave room to swap an outdoor day with an indoor one.
