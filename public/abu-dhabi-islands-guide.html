Abu Dhabi’s islands: choose the right one

Saadiyat, Yas, Jubail and Hudayriyat work as outings from Abu Dhabi by road. Sir Bani Yas is a much longer journey with a transfer to arrange. An island on a map is not automatically open for a casual visit.

Reviewed 30 October 2026

Four islands with different kinds of days

Saadiyat suits museums and beach time. Choose your beach venue before travelling, because hotel access, paid clubs and public facilities are different. Yas is the park-and-entertainment option. Jubail suits a planned mangrove outing, with boardwalk or water activities booked through the operator. Hudayriyat has sporting and waterfront attractions.

Our suggestion is to choose the day first, then the island. A museum morning and a beach afternoon can work on Saadiyat. A park day on Yas is already a full programme. Mangrove kayaking needs its own weather and tide arrangements.

Sir Bani Yas and Dalma need more planning

Sir Bani Yas lies well west of Abu Dhabi city. Its wildlife experiences and island accommodation require coordinated transport and bookings; we would treat it as a separate stay. Dalma also needs a ferry or other confirmed transport arrangement. Check the current service and accommodation before building either into a route.

Do not interpret promotional photographs of smaller islands as permission to land, camp or kayak there independently. Some islands have conservation restrictions or private access.

When to plan an island day

Cooler months make beach walks and mangrove outings more comfortable. Choose a morning or later-afternoon outdoor slot where the operator’s schedule allows it; tides and sea conditions take priority over a preferred photo time.

For seven days split between Dubai and Abu Dhabi, use one of the nearby islands. Our two-week route still leaves Sir Bani Yas as a swap requiring extra nights, rather than promising every island in one trip. See the city guide for museum and mosque pairings.
