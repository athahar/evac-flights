Dubai Frame: views of old and new Dubai

Dubai Frame is a Zabeel Park outing with a raised viewing bridge and exhibitions. Choose it for the contrast between older neighbourhoods and the newer skyline.

Reviewed 30 October 2026

What you are going for

The Frame combines a viewing bridge with displays about Dubai’s past and future. It offers a different perspective from standing among the Downtown towers. The glass section of the walkway may matter if someone in your group is uneasy with heights.

If you only want one paid view, compare this with Burj Khalifa or The View at The Palm. You do not need all three to understand the city.

Pick a clear daylight window

Our suggestion is a clear morning or late afternoon. Daylight helps you recognise the neighbourhoods on either side. In the cooler months, leave time for the park; in summer, reduce the outside walking.

Check entry times and the designated park gate on the current booking page. Do not assume every Zabeel Park entrance takes you directly to the Frame.

Where it fits

This is a flexible extra after Old Dubai, with a separate transfer. It is also an alternative indoor-focused visit while the Museum of the Future is closed.

Allow more breathing room if travelling with children, and check storage arrangements for bags and strollers with the venue.
