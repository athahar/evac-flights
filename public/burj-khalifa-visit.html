Burj Khalifa: choosing a ticket and a time

Choose the observation experience before choosing a time. A higher floor, a sunset slot and a faster entry queue are different things, and one ticket does not necessarily include all three.

Reviewed 30 October 2026

Which ticket do you need?

The official At the Top booking site separates its observation experiences by access level. Compare the floors, included refreshments, queue arrangements and admission time on the exact ticket. A name such as “premium” is less useful than the inclusions.

For a first visit, decide how much of your budget you want to spend on a view. You can also see the tower from outside without buying an observation-deck ticket.

Daylight, sunset or after dark?

Our suggestion: daylight if you want to pick out buildings and the coastline; the period around sunset if seeing the light change matters most. A clear day matters more than a famous time slot. Haze can soften the view in any season.

Keep the outdoor part of your Downtown walk for the cooler hours, especially outside November to March. Reserve the tower time first, then leave space around it for entry queues and the lift journey.

Make it a Downtown day

Use the entrance and arrival instructions on your booking. Allow for the walk through Dubai Mall; arriving at the Metro station is not the same as reaching the ticket desk.

Combine the tower with a meal and a walk around the lake. Check the current fountain programme before making it the centrepiece of your evening. This is the Downtown day in our five-day itinerary.
