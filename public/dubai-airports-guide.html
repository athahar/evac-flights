Dubai travel guide/Airports & flights

DXB or DWC? Check your Dubai airport

DXB is Dubai International. DWC is Dubai World Central, also called Al Maktoum International. They are separate airports, so an airport change needs its own transfer plan.

Reviewed 30 October 2026

Read the airport code, then the terminal

CodeAirportWhat to check
DXBDubai InternationalTerminal 1, 2 or 3, as shown on your booking.
DWCDubai World Central / Al Maktoum InternationalGround transport for DWC, not a DXB terminal transfer.

Check each leg of a return trip separately. A search result labelled “Dubai” does not establish that every flight uses the same airport.

Changing airports is not an airside connection

Dubai Airports places DWC approximately 60 km from DXB. A transfer between them involves entering the UAE, collecting baggage where required, ground travel and another check-in.

Ask the airline or booking provider how much connection time your itinerary needs. Account for entry requirements, traffic and baggage; do not judge the connection using driving time alone.

Before paying for a flight

  • Check the airport code and terminal for arrival and departure.
  • Check whether connecting flights are on one booking or separate tickets.
  • Read baggage allowances and transfer arrangements.
  • Check your hotel transfer for the correct airport.
  • Verify the latest schedule directly with the airline.
